#Fabulous Day Map

 Just a simple map showing a route of places I frequently went in past years, starting at my old apartment complex, and ending at the Taco Bell. It contains 5 points as I added an extra one to show the starting point more clearly, even if it just in the general area of my old apartment.   
###Some Sources
Basemap was provided by Leaflet
I linked to the Watauga Democrat article about the Taco Bell being demolished as I needed to have a link for the requirements, and embedding it made the most sense.
This project was made using primarily HTML with heavy use of the geojson.io website for the making of the features.
I think I technically have the line feature loaded twice, as I was struggling to get the coloring to change for a bit, I think the pink is a good bold choice to show the route.
